# Tile Painter 3D - Quick Start Guide

Welcome to Tile Painter 3D! This lightweight and intuitive tool allows you to build modular 3D environments directly inside the Unity Scene view using a custom grid system and smart auto-tiling.

## Getting Started

1. **Setup the Component**: 
   - Add the `TilePainter3D` component to any GameObject in your scene.
   - Configure your base grid settings (such as `Cell Size` and assign your `Selected Block` from your block library).

2. **Open the Editor Tools**:
   - Select your GameObject with the `TilePainter3D` component.
   - In the Inspector, you will find the **Block Library** preview window and the **3D Paint Tools** control panel.

## How to Paint & Erase

1. **Activate Paint Mode**: 
   - Click the **Paint** button in the Inspector. The button will turn green to indicate active mode.
2. **Paint on the Grid**:
   - Hover your mouse over the visual grid in the Scene view. A cyan wireframe cube will show you where the block will be placed.
   - **Left-Click** or **Click and Drag** on the grid to place tiles.
   - **Change Heights**: Use your mouse **Scroll Wheel** while hovering in the Scene view to move up and down through floors/height levels instantly.
3. **Activate Erase Mode**:
   - Click the **Erase** button (it will turn red). 
   - Click on any existing block in the scene to remove it.

## Additional Controls

- **Undo / Redo**: Use the built-in `Undo` and `Redo` buttons in the Inspector to step backward or forward through your building history seamlessly.
- **Clear Grid**: Use the **Clear Grid** button (with safety confirmation) to wipe the entire grid workspace clean when needed.
