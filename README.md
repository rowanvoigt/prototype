# Project Insights 2026-09-17
## What are we making?
We are making both the building mechanic and points mechanic for a "build your own platformer" style game.
## What question are we trying to answer with our prototype?
We would like to find out if a self-built platformer can be engaging and reward ingenuity.
### Why?
Understanding how the we can create and environment for the player in which they want to engage, think, and test themselves could give us plenty of experience when going into the Game Design field.
## What kind of experience do we envision?
The experience we envision is one where a player chooses the difficulty of challenge for themselves in order to complete the level, with a necessary amount of points required to beat the level.
By giving players the freedom to choose whatever blocks and traps they interact with to gain points, we can then reward ingenuity through many different factors such as variety, difficulty, time to finish, and distance started from the goal.
## How is it innovative or experimental?
The idea is certainly both experimental and innovative as it should require you to gauge your own creativity/skill, and adjust your route building to accommodate for your skillset.
